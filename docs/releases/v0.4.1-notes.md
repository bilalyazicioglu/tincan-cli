# v0.4.1

Use more of tincan with a mouse, keep useful controls in small terminals, and
reach audio shortcuts without relying on F keys.

- Click channels to read their chat while keeping your current voice call.
  Click people to select their volume controls, footer shortcuts to activate
  them, and audio settings to choose devices or adjust microphone controls. (#192)
- Compact layouts retain your call count and mute/deafen state, prioritize
  actionable shortcuts, and keep the focused audio setting accessible. (#193)
- Use Ctrl+Y for the invite, Ctrl+G for talk, Ctrl+T for mute, Ctrl+P for
  push-to-talk, Ctrl+E for deafen and Ctrl+O for audio settings. F-key shortcuts
  still work; macOS footer hints show their Ctrl alternatives. (#194)
- Correct the in-app `/help` shortcut descriptions.

Thanks to **@saimskywalker (Setyo Aji Imam Maliki)** for the terminal shortcut
improvements in [#194](https://github.com/bilalyazicioglu/tincan-cli/pull/194).

Push-to-talk remains a toggle. To select terminal text while mouse capture is
active, use your terminal's selection modifier (often Shift or Option).
